# MYP 56 — Support

Thanks for using **MYP 56**, a study companion for IB Middle Years Programme
students.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need an account?**
No. MYP 56 works completely offline with no sign-up. Everything is stored on
your device.

**Where is my data stored?**
Entirely on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server. See the
[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md).

**How do I back up my Personal Project journal?**
Open **Personal Project → Export**, enter your name, and tap **Download PDF**.
Save that PDF somewhere safe (email it to yourself, save to Files, etc.).

**I deleted the app / cleared my data — can I get it back?**
No. Because nothing is stored online, data removed from your device cannot be
recovered. Export your PDF regularly as a backup.

**The level descriptors / command term explanations — are these official IB
rubrics?**
No. They are plain-language study aids to help you self-assess. Your teacher's
task-specific instructions and the official IB guides always take priority.
